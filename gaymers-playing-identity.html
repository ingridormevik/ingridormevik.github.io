How To Play
Click any building to send your Sim there — Club, Community Center, Thrift, Safe Space, Chat Zone or Waterfront.

Every place changes your needs differently. Let them run low too long and mood drops.

Turn Free Will on and your Sim decides for herself when you stop clicking.

Thrift sometimes hands you a random item. Club, Chat Zone and Community build relationships with the people who hang out there.

Use MAKE YOUR CHARACTER to set your name, pronouns and photo — it becomes your portrait and your pixel face in the room.
