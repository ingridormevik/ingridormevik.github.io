BIFF / AFTER VIRTUAL REALITY / 2 MINUTES

A story
you can step inside.

15–21 October 2026Visningsrommet USF Verftet · Free admissionOpening: 15 October at 17:0016–21 October, 13:00–20:00

The exhibition presents narrative art in VR, AR and XR. See how a room, a screen or an action can become part of a story.

What can you experience?

The Grand Hotel Bald Cockatoo, by Scott Rettberg, Caitlin Fisher and Roderick Coover: dream narratives inside an installation shaped like a bird’s nest. A button gives the visitor a printed fortune.

Between Page and Screen, by Amaranth Borsuk and Brad Bouse, is also in the main exhibition.

XR Demo Days take place on 16–17 October. The demos, performances and Finding Frida require separate registration.
