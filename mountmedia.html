We Built Something That Learns

I did not set out to create a genre. I set out to make something that felt true.

Bergen gave me mountains and rain and darkness nine months of the year. Norwegian folk music gave me melodies that have lived in this land for centuries. Artificial intelligence gave me a collaborator that does not sleep.

From those three things, something emerged.

Intelligence without accountability is just acceleration.

This is not AI making music. This is a person shaping a system.
