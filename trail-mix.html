Trail Mix

A Hiking Story

Interactive Fiction | Two Characters, One Trail | 2026

This one didn't start as a project. It started as an actual hike — Jack a few steps ahead with a DJ controller he insisted on carrying up a mountain, me a few steps behind with a speaker strapped to my pack like an oversized snail shell. Somewhere around the second switchback it became obvious this was happening at the top whether the trail agreed to it or not.

So I turned us into characters. Not as a metaphor for anything — just because it was a good story, and because turning a hike into something playable felt like the most honest way to keep it.

Trail Mix is short: five stops on the way up, a choice at each one, and a summit set waiting at the end. Nothing you pick can go wrong — every path gets you to the top. The only thing that changes is what kind of set is waiting there for you.
