Running in the Mountains Pt. 2

A guided journey through sound & landscape

Mount Media | Pixel-Art Narrative Hike | Bergen, 2026

This started as an actual hike. Jack a few steps ahead with a DJ controller he insisted on carrying up a mountain, me behind him with a speaker strapped to my pack like an oversized snail shell. Somewhere around the second switchback it became obvious this was happening at the top whether the trail agreed to it or not.

So I turned us into characters. Jack is the Trail DJ — he drops the soundtrack and reads the path the way he reads a room. I'm the Sound Carrier — I walk behind, carry the vibe, and keep the battery percentage in my head at all times.

Six chapters, from the car park to the dark walk down. Hold to walk. The mountain does the rest.
