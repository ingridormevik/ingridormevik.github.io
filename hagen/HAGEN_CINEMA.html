Cinema

A scripted 77 second cut of the garden, driven by the track you load. The audio goes through the same spectrum and envelope analysers the installation uses for a microphone or a shared tab, so what you see is the real reactivity, not a re-creation of it.

1 · The track

Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored. The file is read in the browser and released when you close the page.

2 · The cut

Four segments of the source, joined by 1.2 second crossfades. Two audio decks play in turn so no join clicks.

3 · Quality

Pick the ceiling your machine actually holds. If frames start arriving late the engine drops quality on its own and climbs back, which is visible in a recording. Balanced is the safe choice for a first take.

4 · Roll

Preview runs the film so you can watch it. Record runs the same film and captures it: the browser asks which surface to share, and you choose this tab. The picture comes from that capture, the sound is taken straight from the audio graph rather than from your speakers, so the recording is clean even if your volume is down.

Press F for fullscreen before you record. Escape or Q stops the film early.

The recording is WebM, VP9 and Opus, which YouTube accepts directly. For an exact 1920 by 1080 upload from a screen that is not 16:9:
ffmpeg -i hagen-cinema.webm -vf "scale=1920:-2,crop=1920:1080" -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -preset slow -c:a aac -b:a 320k hagen-cinema.mp4
