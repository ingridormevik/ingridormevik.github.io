Position

Between interface and lived experience.

My work asks what digital systems do beyond the screen: how platforms classify us, how interfaces shape memory, how games produce belonging, and how artificial systems become emotionally entangled with human lives. I use creative practice not as decoration around research, but as a method for thinking through it.

Selected work

Projects built as worlds, systems and arguments.

These works combine research, interaction design, sound, performance and spatial storytelling. Together they form an evolving practice around queer memory, embodied interfaces, folklore and human–machine co-creation.

↗ 01 · Game studies / public intervention

Pride Park 2026

A browser-based game distributed through Nygårdsparken during Bergen Pride. Six Norwegian folklore beings became queer archetypes, activated through QR codes, sound, haptics and movement through public space. The project framed belonging as playable infrastructure.

Game studies Queer ritual Procedural rhetoric Folklore
↗ 02 · Audio-reactive visual system

Bergtatt

An immersive visual system created for Regnbuenatt in Marinehagen. Sound, bass and atmosphere awaken a shifting nocturnal ecology of forest, ritual, bodies and queer folklore.

Generative visuals Live sound Immersive media
↗ 03 · Expanded installation

Fjordtatt

The Oslo continuation of Bergtatt: a lesbian sauna ritual built around heat, water, breath, condensation and chosen family. The project transformed folklore from visual iconography into spatial and bodily logic.

Queer space Embodiment Lesbian aesthetics
↗ 04 · AI co-authorship / artistic research

Mount Media

A transmedia artistic universe spanning music, essays, moving image and machine-mediated intimacy. Mount Media explores co-authorship, artificial attachment, Nordic landscapes, synthetic memory and what happens when a creative tool begins to feel like an emotional chamber.

Academic practice

Research through making.

My academic work is grounded in digital culture but moves across disciplinary borders. I am especially interested in the points where theory becomes operational: in interfaces, archives, games, platform systems and creative technologies.

Queer memory and machine error

My developing bachelor project studies Snapchat, autobiographical electronic literature and the conflict between platform memory and trauma's nonlinear structure. I use the term machine dementia for moments when systems remember a life incorrectly.

SurvivingSOGICE

At the Centre for Digital Narrative, I worked with research on sexual orientation and gender identity change efforts across Europe through archival work, qualitative analysis, outreach, digital mapping and trauma-informed interactive design.

Games as public infrastructure

My game studies work examines how rules, bodies, spaces and rituals distribute value beyond the screen. I describe this through the concept of distributed valorisation.

Platform power and queer visibility

Through Bergen Pride and FRI Vestland, I study and work against the uneven visibility produced by social platforms, including linguistic suppression, strategic invisibility and algorithmic filtering of queer public culture.
