Arrival and orientation

RECORD1891

Neevengården asylum opens

The institution opened in 1891 under the name Neevengården sinnssykeasyl.

Institusjonen åpnet i 1891 under navnet Neevengården sinnssykeasyl.

Source: Sandviken sykehus, Store norske leksikon · checked 2026-08-11

RECORD1927

Renamed Neevengården sykehus

In 1927 the name changed to Neevengården sykehus. The word for asylum was dropped.

I 1927 ble navnet endret til Neevengården sykehus. «Asyl» falt bort.

Source: Sandviken sykehus, Store norske leksikon · checked 2026-08-11

RECORD1978

Renamed Sandviken sykehus

In 1978 it became Sandviken sykehus — named for a place rather than for what happened inside it. Three names in eighty-seven years, each one moving further from the last. The building did not change. What we were willing to call it did.

I 1978 fikk sykehuset navnet Sandviken sykehus — et stedsnavn, ikke en diagnose.

Source: Sandviken sykehus, Store norske leksikon · checked 2026-08-11
