The Monk

Not attested at this location — Mount Media interpretation.

A rock shape in the old valley that, in the right light, tips into looking like a kneeling figure. Bergen byleksikon records a real local legend attached to it — a hooded monk, a confession, the stream called Gunnhildarå running past. The game never confirms the shape is anything but stone. Encountered by stopping at the right point and letting the light do the work, never by searching for it.

The one being on this route with a documented local legend behind it, not only Mount Media's own reading — still shown as unverified until a human confirms the source (see data/sources.json's munkebotn_munken), same bar as everything else here.
