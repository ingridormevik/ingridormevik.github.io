Trail Mix — Sprite Preview

Every animation built by tools/build-sprites.py from assets/pack/. Nothing here is wired into the game yet. Check the frame boundaries and the cutouts, then say which animations are approved. ← back to the game

Why the sprites were rebuilt. The supplied pack was cut from the concept sheet with a brightness threshold. Ingrid's black shirt and shorts sit within a few levels of that sheet's near-black background, so the pack's own alpha punches holes straight through her clothing (and through Jack's shorts and controller). The pack's RGB survives under that alpha, so the build script ignores the packed alpha and rebuilds it with a sealed flood fill. Toggle Compare with pack alpha below to see the difference.
