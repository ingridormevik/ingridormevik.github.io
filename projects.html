Interactive fiction about AI bias against Norwegian LGBTQ+ organisations — a system that quietly refuses to recognise the people it is asked to describe. Research you move through rather than read.

↗ Location-based game · Bergen Pride 2026

Pride Park 2026: The Forest Remembers

A browser-based game distributed through Nygårdsparken during Bergen Pride. Six Norwegian folklore beings become queer archetypes, activated through QR codes, sound, haptics and movement through public space — belonging as playable infrastructure.
