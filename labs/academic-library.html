Read with a question.

  1. Use the contents or index to locate a relevant chapter. For edited collections, choose a chapter and identify its author.
  2. Read the passage in context. Separate the author's claim from your own interpretation.
  3. Record the edition and actual page or locator you used. Cite a chapter by its author, not automatically by the book's editors.
  4. Use the idea to question a design choice, compare two approaches or develop an argument.

For library access, choose your institution in Oria and check the actual record. A publisher page may offer a preview or require purchase; it is not automatically free full text.
