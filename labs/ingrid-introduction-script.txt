INGRID / FIRST LAB INTRODUCTION

Hi, I'm Ingrid, your lab assistant. I'm a third-year Digital Culture student, which means my knowledge is still compiling. I don't know everything, but I'm good at finding things out. If we hit something I don't know, we can look it up, test it or ask someone who does.

I make electronic literature, games, sound and audiovisual work. I also work across research and activism. In projects like The Turing Test and Mount Media, I often begin with something I have experienced or a question I cannot quite leave alone. Then I ask what form would let another person encounter it.

That's what I would like to help you do. You don't need to copy my subjects, share anything private or use AI. You can begin with a place, a routine, a sound, an irritation—something you've noticed.

These labs are time for your own projects. I'll show a small example, we'll have plenty of working time, and we will take breaks. I'm here to help with ideas, code, finding sources, getting unstuck or working out the question in the first place. This is a workshop, not a speedrun. Let's choose one small thing you want to move forward today.
